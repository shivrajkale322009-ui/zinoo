## Purpose and Scope

This Policy explains cookies and similar storage/tracking technologies used or planned in Zinoo’s mobile app and any future website. Mobile apps more commonly use SDK identifiers, app storage, and device permissions than browser cookies.

## Definitions

“Cookies” are small browser files. “Local storage” includes browser or application storage used to retain settings, sessions, or cached data. “SDKs/pixels” are software components or tags that can transmit events and device/network information.

## Current Mobile Application

Zinoo may use local app storage for authentication state, preferences, performance, or cached content. Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics may use identifiers or local storage to measure use and diagnose crashes. Google Maps may process map/location-related data. Meta Pixel/Facebook Analytics is listed in the project facts but its actual implementation and configuration have not been verified.

A future Zinoo website may use essential cookies for security and functionality and optional cookies or pixels for preferences, analytics, or advertising. ## Managing Technologies

Users may be able to manage browser cookies through browser controls, reset advertising identifiers or privacy choices in device settings, revoke location permission, or clear app data. Blocking technologies may affect functionality. Browser “do not track” signals are not uniformly interpreted.
