Subject to the final lawful-basis and consent design, Zinoo may use information to:

## 4. Information Sharing and Service Providers

### User-directed sharing with sellers

Zinoo does **not** automatically share buyer information with sellers. When a user deliberately selects actions such as **Contact Seller**, **Express Interest**, **Book Site Visit**, or **Submit Inquiry**, Zinoo may disclose the information needed for that request to the relevant seller or contact recipient.

Before submission, the interface should identify the recipient and fields to be shared. The recipient may then contact the user and may process the data under its own notice.

### Technology providers

Depending on configuration, Zinoo uses or plans to use:

- **Firebase Authentication** for phone/OTP authentication;
- **Firebase Firestore** for application data;

These providers may receive identifiers, device/network information, events, content, or location depending on configuration. Their own terms and notices may apply.
