## Purpose

This guide defines how Zinoo versions, dates, publishes, and archives legal documents.

## Scope

It applies to every file in `docs/legal/` and every published copy.

## Version Numbers

Zinoo uses `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`:

- **MAJOR:** breaking legal change affecting rights, obligations, dispute terms, material processing purposes, data sharing, or platform role.
- **MINOR:** material but non-breaking addition, such as a new feature, SDK, data category, or clearer user right.
- **PATCH:** correction or clarification that does not materially change rights or practices.
- **Prerelease:** `-draft` means not approved or effective.

Each public document displays its own Version, Effective Date, and Last Updated date. “Effective Date” is when users become governed by the version. “Last Updated” is the date text was last approved for publication.

## Release Process

Breaking or materially adverse changes require counsel review, a notice plan, and any consent or advance notice required by law. Minor and patch revisions still require factual verification. A release should use one documentation-set version unless a documented reason requires a document-specific version.

## Archives

Before publication, copy the complete superseded set to `archive/<version>/`, retain its dates and changelog, and do not edit it afterward. Never archive drafts as if they were effective releases.
