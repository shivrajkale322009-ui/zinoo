## Purpose and Scope

This Disclaimer explains important limits concerning property information and Zinoo’s marketplace role. It supplements the [Terms](terms-and-conditions.md) and does not remove rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

## Platform Role

Zinoo is a technology marketplace for discovering and communicating about real estate. Unless expressly stated for a specific service, Zinoo is not a seller, property owner, developer, broker, lender, valuer, architect, surveyor, lawyer, financial adviser, escrow provider, or party to a property transaction.

## Property and Third-Party Information

Listings, developer/seller statements, images, reviews, maps, and other third-party materials may be incomplete, outdated, inaccurate, or unavailable. Zinoo does not guarantee:

- seller/developer identity, authority, performance, or responsibility;
- title, ownership, encumbrances, RERA status, permits, approvals, zoning, construction quality, possession dates, or legal compliance;
- price, incentives, cashback, taxes, fees, finance terms, inventory, or continued availability;
- carpet/built-up/plot area, boundaries, floor plans, distances, map pins, routes, neighbourhood information, photographs, renders, or specifications.

Map locations and distances are approximate and may depend on Google Maps Platform, device location, network conditions, or user/seller input.

Projects, layouts, designs, amenities, specifications, timelines, and prices may change subject to law and binding seller/developer documents. Illustrations and renders may be indicative.

## Independent Verification and Risk

Users should conduct site inspections and obtain independent title/legal review, measurements/surveys, technical inspection, valuation, financial/tax advice, and verification from RERA and other relevant authorities before paying money or signing documents. Real-estate decisions carry market, construction, credit, liquidity, regulatory, fraud, and counterparty risk.

Zinoo content and recommendations are general discovery information, not legal, financial, tax, investment, engineering, valuation, or other professional advice. Past market information or popularity does not predict returns or suitability.

Developers, sellers, brokers, and other third parties are responsible for their own Listings, claims, communications, services, and legal duties. Zinoo is responsible for its own conduct only to the extent provided by applicable law.
