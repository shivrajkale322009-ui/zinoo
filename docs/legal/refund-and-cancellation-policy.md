## Purpose and Scope

This Policy accurately records Zinoo’s current pre-launch payment position.

## Current Position

Zinoo does not currently provide a payment gateway or accept payments for property purchases, deposits, bookings, subscriptions, or paid platform services. Therefore, Zinoo currently has no platform payment to refund or cancel.

A “booking request,” “site visit,” “inquiry,” “expression of interest,” saved property, or cashback-history entry within Zinoo is not by itself proof that Zinoo received money or formed a binding property booking.

If a user pays a seller, developer, broker, payment provider, or another third party outside Zinoo, that payment is governed by the agreement and refund/cancellation terms between those parties. Zinoo does not promise, hold, or process such refunds unless it expressly agrees otherwise in a future service. Users should verify the recipient and written terms before paying.

Users may ask to cancel a pending Zinoo request by contacting the relevant recipient and **shivrajkale322009@gmail.com**, but cancellation cannot guarantee that a third party has not already acted on or retained the request as permitted by law.
