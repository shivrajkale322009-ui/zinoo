## Purpose and Scope

This Policy describes the proposed process for deleting a Zinoo account and associated personal data. It supplements the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).

## Definitions

“Deletion” means erasure or irreversible anonymisation, as appropriate. “Account data” includes profile and service data associated with an authenticated user, subject to verified exceptions.

## Requesting Deletion

Until an in-app and web workflow is implemented, a user may email **shivrajkale322009@gmail.com** from a contact channel reasonably associated with the account and include the Zinoo phone number and a clear deletion request. Users should not email OTPs or unnecessary identity documents.

## Proposed Workflow

1. Acknowledge and log the request.
2. Verify control of the account using proportionate, secure authentication.
3. Explain any material effects or lawful exceptions.
4. Disable/delete the account and delete or anonymise covered records from active systems.
5. Send deletion instructions to relevant processors where applicable.
6. Allow protected backups to expire under the approved backup cycle.
7. Confirm completion without exposing sensitive information.

Deletion may affect profile data, search/view history, saved properties, inquiries, booking requests, cashback history, reviews/ratings, images, and associated authentication records. The exact scope must be verified.

## Exceptions and Retained Information

Zinoo may retain limited information where necessary and permitted for legal obligations, security, fraud prevention, dispute or claim handling, enforcement, financial/tax records, suppression of unwanted contact, or protected backups. Retained information should be access-restricted and deleted when the reason ends. Content already shared with a seller or WhatsApp recipient may be held independently by that recipient.
